vprepcaa

VCAA exam practice for Victorian students

vprepcaa is a free practice site for Victorian Certificate of Education (VCE) students. Students work through past VCAA exam questions by topic, mark their own work, and see which areas need more practice. Teachers can see how their classes are progressing and set work.

Who it is for

Students and teachers at Victorian secondary schools. Students sign in with a Google account provided by their school or their own.

Status

The site is being rebuilt and is not yet open for general sign-in. Access is currently limited to students of the teacher who runs it.
