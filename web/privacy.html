Privacy policy

Draft, last updated 18 November 2026. vprepcaa is run by Andy, a secondary school mathematics teacher in Victoria, Australia.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and Google account ID. We use the basic sign-in permissions only (openid, email, profile). We never ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, Classroom or any other Google data.

While you use the site we store the practice you do: which questions you attempted, your self-marked scores, and the dates. If you join a class, your teacher can see this for that class.

What we use it for

To sign you in, save your progress between devices and show you and your teacher how practice is going. We do not sell your information, show advertising, or use it for anything else.

Who can see it

You can see your own data. Teachers can see the data of students in their own classes. The site administrator can see all data in order to run the service. We do not share personal information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Cloudflare, Inc. Cloudflare may store and process it in data centres outside Australia.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by contacting the address below. Deleting your account removes your progress.
